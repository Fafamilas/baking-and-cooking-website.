Butter cookies

butter cookies is asimple recipe made from butter and flour.

procedure

1.put 500g of butter in a bowl and add 250g of sugarand mix well till you get afluffy mixture.
2.Add two eggs and whisk well,berore adding 1tsp of vanilla essence.
3.Add 5oog of flour ang mix well to form a dough.
4.roll the dough into a big circle and use acookie cutter to cut to different shapes.
5.place the cookies in a baking tray and bake in a preaheated oven at 180degrees for 15-20 minutes or until golden brown.
6.remove from the oven and let it cool for five minutes before serving.
7.serve with a cup of tea or coffee and enjoy your delicious butter cookies.

Chocolate chip cookies

chocolate chip cookies is a classic cookie recipe made with butter,sugar,eggs and chocolate chips.

steps

1.preheat the oven to 180degrees celcius and line a baking tray with parchment paper.
2.in a bowl,cream together 200g of butter and 150g of sugar until light and fluffy.
3.add two eggs and 1tsp of vanilla essence and mix well.
4.add 300g of flour and 1tsp of baking soda and mix the dough until well combined.
5.add 200g of chocolate chips and mix until evenly distributed.
6. use aspoon to scoop the dough onto the prepared baking tray, leaving some spaces between each cookie.
7.bake in a preheated oven for 10-12 minutes or until the edges are golden brown.
8.remove from oven and let them cool for a few minutes before serving,enjoy your delicious cookies with a glass of milk or a cup of coffee.
